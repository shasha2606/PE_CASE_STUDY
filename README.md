# Retail Sales & Promotional Performance Dataset

## 1. Dataset Overview

This dataset contains historical retail sales and promotional performance information across multiple products, brands, manufacturers, retailers, and market regions.

The dataset is designed to support analytical exercises related to **Revenue Growth Management (RGM)**, particularly **Price Elasticity (PE)** 

It provides information on product-level sales performance, promotional activity, and weighted distribution, enabling users to investigate the relationship between pricing, promotions, product availability, and consumer demand.

## 2. Dataset Structure

Each observation represents the sales and distribution performance of a particular product (SKU) within a specific retailer, market region, and reporting period.

The dataset contains the following variables:

### A. Product & Market Information

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `MKT_Region` | Geographic market or region associated with the sales observation. |
| `RETAILER` | Retail chain or retailer where the product is sold. |
| `SKU` | Stock Keeping Unit — unique identifier for an individual product. |
| `PPG` | Product Price Group — grouping of products with similar pricing or product characteristics. |
| `Format` | Product packaging or format classification. |
| `Manufacturer` | Company responsible for manufacturing the product. |
| `Brand` | Brand under which the product is marketed. |
| `Sub Category` | Product classification within a broader category. |
| `Size (ml)` | Product pack size measured in millilitres. |
| `Date` | Reporting period associated with the sales observation. |

### B. Sales Performance Metrics

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `FCT_Units` | Total number of product units sold during the reporting period. |
| `FCT_Units (any promo)` | Number of units sold under any promotional activity. |
| `FCT_Volume` | Total product volume sold during the reporting period. |
| `FCT_Volume (any promo)` | Product volume sold under promotional activity. |
| `FCT_Value` | Total sales revenue generated by the product during the reporting period. |
| `FCT_Value (any promo)` | Sales revenue generated from promotional sales. |

### C. Distribution Metrics

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `FCT_Weight. Distribution (w)` | Weighted distribution, representing product availability across retail outlets, adjusted for the relative sales importance of those outlets. |
| `FCT_Weight. Distribution (w) (any promo)` | Weighted distribution associated with outlets where the product was sold under promotional activity. |

*Note: The promotional weighted-distribution column may contain additional spaces in the original file header.*

## 3. Key Business Concepts

**Sales Units:** Represents the quantity of products sold and is an important measure of consumer demand.

**Sales Value:** Represents the monetary value of product sales and can be used alongside units to calculate average realized selling prices.

**Promotional Sales:** Refers to sales associated with promotional activities, such as temporary price reductions, special offers, or other promotional mechanics.

**Weighted Distribution:** Measures product availability while accounting for the sales importance of stores, helping distinguish changes in consumer demand from changes in retail availability.

**Price Elasticity:** Measures the responsiveness of consumer demand to changes in product prices.

**Post Event Analytics:** Evaluates historical promotional performance to understand sales outcomes, promotional effectiveness, and potential incremental business impact.

## 4. Potential Analytical Applications

The dataset can support several business analyses, including:

1. **Sales Trend Analysis:** Evaluate historical sales patterns across products, brands, retailers, and regions.
2. **Price Analysis:** Calculate average realized selling prices and investigate relationships between price movements and sales volumes.
3. **Promotional Performance:** Assess promotional sales contribution and compare performance across products and retailers.
4. **Distribution Analysis:** Examine how product availability and weighted distribution relate to sales performance.
5. **Price Elasticity Exploration:** Investigate the sensitivity of consumer demand to price changes while considering promotional and distribution effects.

## 5. Important Considerations

- Average realized selling price can be derived by dividing `FCT_Value` by `FCT_Units`, where units are nonzero.
- Promotional sales metrics represent a subset of total sales, rather than additional sales.
- The presence of promotional sales does not necessarily indicate that all units sold during a reporting period were promoted.
- Weighted distribution should be interpreted according to the measurement scale used in the dataset.
- Product size and volume units should be validated before comparing products across formats.
- Observed relationships between price, promotions, and sales should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects.
- Promotional uplift and incremental sales cannot be directly inferred without establishing an appropriate baseline.

## 6. Intended Usage

This dataset is intended for educational, analytical, and case-study purposes. It allows analysts to demonstrate their ability to perform exploratory data analysis, derive meaningful business metrics, identify performance drivers, and translate analytical findings into actionable commercial recommendations.

The primary objective is to assess both **technical analytical capabilities** and **business understanding** within a retail and FMCG environment.
